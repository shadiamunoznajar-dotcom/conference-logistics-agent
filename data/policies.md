# Speaker policies / Políticas para speakers
Antara Conference at Harvard 2027 (fictional). The agent may answer a speaker's question ONLY if the answer is written in this file. Anything else goes to the "questions for a human" list.

## Travel categories / Categorías de viaje

| Code | English | Español |
|---|---|---|
| A | Flight booked and paid by the organizers. Hotel covered. | Vuelo comprado por la organización. Hotel cubierto. |
| B | Speaker books their own flight; organizers reimburse it. Hotel covered. | El/la speaker compra su vuelo y se le reembolsa. Hotel cubierto. |
| C | Speaker pays their own flight and hotel. | El/la speaker cubre su vuelo y su hotel. |
| L | Local speaker, no travel. | Speaker local, sin viaje. |

## Flights / Vuelos
- **Category A:** economy fare that includes a carry-on and one personal item only. A checked bag is at the speaker's own cost; they can add it on the AeroAntara website with their booking code. / Tarifa económica que incluye solo equipaje de mano y un artículo personal. La maleta documentada corre por cuenta del speaker; puede agregarla en la web de AeroAntara con su código de reserva.
- **Category A flight changes** must be made by the organizers. If the airline reschedules a speaker, the speaker should forward the new itinerary. / Los cambios de vuelo de la categoría A los hace la organización. Si la aerolínea reprograma el vuelo, el speaker debe reenviar el nuevo itinerario.
- **Category B reimbursement:** up to US$900 round trip. Requires BOTH (1) the ticket/itinerary showing the passenger name and (2) proof of payment. Reimbursement is processed after the conference. / Hasta US$900 ida y vuelta. Requiere AMBOS: (1) el boleto o itinerario con el nombre del pasajero y (2) el comprobante de pago. El reembolso se procesa después de la conferencia.

## Hotel / Alojamiento
- Categories A and B: up to **2 nights** at the Charles River Grand Hotel (88 Riverside Way, Cambridge), on the nights the speaker needs for the conference. At check-in, show a passport and say you are with "Antara Conference at Harvard". / Hasta **2 noches** en el Charles River Grand Hotel, en las noches que el speaker necesite para la conferencia. Al llegar, presentar pasaporte y mencionar "Antara Conference at Harvard".
- Extra nights are at the speaker's own cost and are arranged directly with the hotel. / Las noches adicionales corren por cuenta del speaker y se coordinan directamente con el hotel.
- Standard check-in is 3:00 PM. The organizers can **request** early check-in (not guaranteed) for anyone arriving at the hotel before 3:00 PM. / El check-in es a las 3:00 PM. La organización puede **solicitar** early check-in (no garantizado) si el speaker llega antes de las 3:00 PM.
- The organizers send the hotel a rooming list; any change in dates must be sent to the hotel by the organizers. / La organización envía la lista de huéspedes al hotel; cualquier cambio de fechas lo comunica la organización.

## Ground transport / Traslados
- All traveling speakers (A, B, C): taxi/Uber/Lyft between the airport, hotel and venues is reimbursed up to **US$100 total**. **No receipts are needed** for this amount. / Taxi/Uber/Lyft entre aeropuerto, hotel y sedes se reembolsa hasta **US$100 en total**. **No se necesitan comprobantes** para este monto.
- Logan Airport has free wifi ("Logan Airport"). / El aeropuerto Logan tiene wifi gratuito ("Logan Airport").

## Event details / Detalles del evento
- Dress code: business casual, both days. / Código de vestimenta: business casual, ambos días.
- Call time: speakers arrive at the venue **30 minutes before** their session. / Los speakers llegan **30 minutos antes** de su sesión.
- Session language is listed in the program for each session. / El idioma de cada sesión figura en el programa.
- Slides: send by **Thursday April 15, 6:00 PM**. Large files can be sent as a download link. / Enviar las diapositivas hasta el **jueves 15 de abril, 6:00 PM**. Archivos pesados pueden enviarse como enlace.
- The full program with times is attached to every logistics email as `program_antara_2027.pdf`. / El programa completo con horarios va adjunto como `program_antara_2027.pdf`.
- Speaker breakfast: Friday April 16, 9:30–10:30 AM, Dunmore Hall Dining Room. RSVP needed, plus any allergies or dietary restrictions. / Desayuno de speakers: viernes 16 de abril, 9:30–10:30 AM. Confirmar asistencia e indicar alergias o restricciones.
- Networking dinner: Friday April 16, 7:00–9:00 PM. Speakers only (guests/companions are not covered by this policy). / Cena de networking: solo speakers (los acompañantes no están contemplados en esta política).

## What every speaker must send / Lo que cada speaker debe enviar
Due Tuesday April 13 (slides: Thursday April 15, 6:00 PM).

| Item | Who |
|---|---|
| Short bio (≤120 words) / Bio corta | Everyone |
| Headshot / Foto | Everyone |
| Allergies or dietary restrictions ("none" is a valid answer) / Alergias o restricciones | Everyone |
| Speaker breakfast RSVP / Confirmación del desayuno | Everyone |
| AV needs ("none" is a valid answer) / Necesidades de AV | Everyone |
| Arrival date and time in Boston / Fecha y hora de llegada | Categories B and C (Category A is already known from the booking) |
| Flight ticket + proof of payment / Boleto + comprobante de pago | Category B only |
| Slides | Anyone presenting slides (keynotes always; panelists only if they say they will) |

## Not covered by this file → ask a human
Anything not written above, for example: bringing guests to the breakfast or dinner, per diems, upgrades, changes to session length or format, honoraria, visa letters.
